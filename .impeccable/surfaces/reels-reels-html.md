# Quick Reels — Surface Brief

## Scope and visitor mode

- Route: `/reels/`
- Subject: Business Intelligence pilot (IT 249), all six units
- Mode: Read inside an integrated study tool
- Source of truth: the current `study-site-v2/01-business-intelligence/` Markdown files

## Confirmed audience, job, and constraints

BIM students revise from the exact wording already published on the site. They need to understand one source passage at a time, hear it if useful, move manually to the next passage, and retain Seen, Done, Saved, and resume state. Educational wording, order within source passages, tables, and diagrams must not be rewritten. Question cards show the question before the answer. Audio is browser TTS for text and local MP3 music for visual-only cards.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

A focused study reel: one centred portrait source frame on a pale stage, with a restrained icon rail at the lower-right edge and every secondary control hidden behind a modal drawer. The memorable moment is entering any card and hearing the exact question first, a short pause, then the exact answer, while the text remains fully visible. This borrows a reel's spatial rhythm without becoming a social-media screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Exact exam-note content becomes a source-linked, narrated study reel without fake social metrics, autoplay, or a permanent dashboard shell.

OWN-WORLD: Newsreader display type, system sans for controls, the existing BI blue as the only chroma, a pale solid stage, a white portrait reading surface, and ink-filled circular study actions.

STORY: The learner always knows the unit, source position, progress, and whether the current card is spoken or visual. One deliberate action advances the reading; every card can return to its exact unit section.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A quiet utility masthead above one centred 9:16 study frame. Source text fills the frame; Listen, Source, Save, and Done stack at the lower-right edge, with previous/next chevrons beneath them. No permanent sidebar or visible control wall.

FORM: Brief-pinned vertical carousel reading stage; seed:4a47ba9ccbd965d2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
