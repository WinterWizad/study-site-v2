## The three things that decide this paper

1. **Definitions at length.** Group A is 2 marks each — not 1 mark. You write ~55 words, not ~25. Every unit file opens with the definition in writeable form at that length.
2. **Two diagrams you must draw cold.** The **Layered Internet Model** (A6, B14/B15 context) and the **Value Creation Model** (C19). Under 90 seconds each, or the marks are gone.
3. **The case is compulsory and Nepali.** Unit 6 owns the 20-mark case. The answer must cite the six case facts (reach, price transparency, data reliability, funding dependency, digital divide, policy) and use Unit 1/4 concepts (4IR, digital transformation drivers). No generic case answer passes.
4. **Unit 5 is thin on marks (12) but C21 is the only analytical 10-marker.** You cannot bluff it from definitions — it needs the asymmetric-information machinery and the AI/labour-market link.
