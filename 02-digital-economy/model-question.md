This is the verbatim 2026 board paper for IT 250 Digital Economy. No official model question was issued for this subject; this board paper serves as the reference.

## The 2026 Board Paper (verbatim, by group)

**IT 250: Digital Economy** · Full Marks 100 · Pass Marks 50

### Group A — Brief answer [10 × 2 = 20]

1. What are the drivers of the digital economy?
2. What is the K-economy?
3. What is meant by digital monopolization?
4. List out any four types of multi-sided platforms.
5. What are the sources of switching costs?
6. State the Layered Internet Model.
7. What are the characteristics of digital innovation?
8. Mention the types of digital currency.
9. Write any two digital challenges to traditional IP frameworks.
10. What is e-governance?

### Group B — Short answer (attempt any SIX) [6 × 5 = 30]

11. Explain the ecosystem of digital economics with examples.
12. Describe the dimensions of the OECD Digital Government index.
13. Define network effects. Why are they important in digital markets? Explain.
14. Differentiate between competition and coopetition with examples.
15. Explain the data-driven business models with examples.
16. How does technological revolution affect globalization and economic growth? Explain.
17. Explain the regulatory challenges of digital currencies.

### Group C — Long answer (attempt any THREE) [3 × 10 = 30]

18. Evaluate the opportunities and challenges of the fourth industrial revolution.
19. Explain the value creation model. What are its economic implications?
20. What is digital transformation? Explain its drivers.
21. Analyze the effects of digitalization on consumer choice and labour markets.

### Group D — Case analysis [20]

22. **Agro-tech Concern** — a web application launched in 2080 BS by three agriculture graduates and two computer programmers in Chitwan, serving small farmers with weather advisories, a real-time market-price dashboard, a direct marketplace and peer-to-peer knowledge sharing. Two years on: 85,555 registered farmers, NPR 489 million in direct transactions, a 16% average price gain for dashboard users, but near-zero reach in remote hill and mountain districts, manual price collection with errors, and a funding model dependent on UNDP, SDC and ministry subsidies earning only NPR 16 million a year in fees.

> a. Identify the major issues in the given case.
> b. Evaluate the opportunities and challenges of digital transformation in the agriculture sector in Nepal using the example of Agro-tech Concern.
> c. Suggest the sustainable revenue model that can be followed by Agro-tech Concern to support farmers in the digitalization of agri-business.
> d. What types of policy measures would you suggest to the Ministry of Agriculture for enabling e-governance platforms to be effectively implemented in agri-business digitalization?

*(The case narrative is condensed here; the full verbatim text is in the raw source file. Do not learn the condensation — learn the six facts it turns on: reach, price transparency, data reliability, funding dependency, digital divide, and policy.)*
