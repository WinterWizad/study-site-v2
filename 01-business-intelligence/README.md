## The three things that decide this paper

1. **Definitions, exactly.** Group A is 10 free marks of pure definition recall. Every unit file opens with the definition in writeable form.
2. **Two diagrams you must be able to draw cold.** The data warehouse architecture (5-marker, and it underpins the ETL 3-marker) and the star schema (3-marker). Under 90 seconds each, or the marks are gone.
3. **Group D case method — rehearsed in both shapes.** One 20-mark Amazon-style case with four sub-questions (board paper) *and* two 10-mark cases with no choice (model question). No exceptions on either.
