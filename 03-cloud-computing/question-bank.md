| Unit | Topic | Hours | 1-markers | 3-markers | 5-markers | Case (10) | Total marks |

| 3 | Cloud Applications | 5 | — | — | — | — | **0** |
| 4 | Virtualization Technology | 8 | A6 | B13, B14 | — | D21 | **17** |
| 5 | MapReduce and Applications | 7 | A8 | B15 | C19 | — | **9** |
| 6 | Cloud Security | 6 | A7, A9 | B16 | C18, C20 | D22 | **25** |
| 7 | Cloud Platforms and Applications | 12 | A10 | — | — | — | **1** |

**Unit 6 (Security) is 25/60 marks** — the case engine.
**Unit 4 (Virtualization) is 17/60 marks** — the diagram engine.
**Units 6 + 4 = 42/60 marks = 70% of the paper from 14/48 hours = 29% of syllabus.**

Unit 3 is absent from the model question but a board setter could easily put a 5-marker on migration/SLA/cost. Unit 7 is 12 hours for 1 mark (AppEngine) — know the names and one-line descriptions, do not deep-dive.
