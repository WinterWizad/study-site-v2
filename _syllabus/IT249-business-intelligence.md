## Course objective

Enable students to understand the role and importance of Business Intelligence in modern organisations; learn key concepts and techniques in data warehousing, data mining and data visualisation; gain hands-on familiarity with popular BI tools; and develop the skill of analysing and interpreting data to support decision-making.

## Course description

Introduces the concepts, techniques and tools used in business intelligence and data analytics, and how BI transforms data into insights for organisational decision-making.

## Units

### Unit 1 — Introduction to Business Intelligence · 5 hrs
- Definition and scope of BI
- Importance of BI in decision-making
- Overview of BI tools and technologies

### Unit 2 — Data Warehousing · 8 hrs
- Introduction to data warehousing concepts
- Data warehouse architecture
- ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes
- Data modelling for BI

### Unit 3 — Data Mining · 8 hrs
- Introduction to data mining concepts
- Data preprocessing techniques
- Classification and clustering algorithms
- Association rule mining

### Unit 4 — Data Visualisation · 7 hrs
- Principles of data visualisation
- Tools and techniques for data visualisation
- Dashboard design and best practices

### Unit 5 — BI Tools and Technologies · 12 hrs (largest unit)
- Overview of popular BI tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Hands-on experience with BI tools for analysis and reporting
- Introduction to OLAP (Online Analytical Processing)

### Unit 6 — BI Implementation · 8 hrs
- Best practices for implementing BI solutions
- Challenges and considerations in BI implementation
- Case studies of successful BI implementations

## Laboratory work

Lab covers hands-on use of BI tools and technologies, plus implementing data warehousing, data mining and data visualisation techniques. **Out of scope for this prep** (theory papers only) — but lab vocabulary shows up in 1-mark and case questions, so it is covered in the notes as concept, not as tooling practice.

## Suggested readings

- Sherman, R. (2014). *Business Intelligence Guidebook: From Data Integration to Analytics.* Morgan Kaufmann.
- Provost, F. & Fawcett, T. (2013). *Data Science for Business.* O'Reilly.
- Kimball, R. & Ross, M. (2013). *The Data Warehouse Toolkit: The Definitive Guide to Dimensional Modeling.* Wiley.

## How the 48 hours should be read

Unit 5 (12 hrs) and Units 2–3 (16 hrs combined) are half the course. If time collapses, Unit 5 + Unit 2 + the Group D method are the highest-value block — Unit 5 carries the most questions across the paper and Unit 2 carries the guaranteed architecture question.
