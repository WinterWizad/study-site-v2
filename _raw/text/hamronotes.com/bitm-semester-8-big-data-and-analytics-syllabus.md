Full Marks: 60

Pass Marks: 30

Credit Hours: 3

## Course Description

The course covers different concepts of big data technologies, including an overview and challenges of big data, big data analytics, Hadoop and NoSQL systems, data lakes, and big data applications.

## Course Objective

The objective of this course is to provide knowledge of big data and related concepts to students. This course is designed to help students learn big data technologies, NoSQL systems, data lakes, and their applications.
