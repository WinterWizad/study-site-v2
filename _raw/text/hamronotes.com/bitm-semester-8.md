## About BITM Semester 8

BITM Semester 8 notes on HamroNotes cover all 7 subjects of the TU curriculum: IT Entrepreneurship and Management (IT248), Business Intelligence (IT249), Digital Economy (IT250), Dot Net Programming (IT275), Database Administration (IT276), Cloud Computing (IT277), Big Data and Analytics (IT278). Every subject is organised chapter by chapter — read the notes online, practise solved chapter-wise questions, and revise from question banks built on past exam papers.

Open a subject above to browse its chapters, or go straight to its question bank when exams are close.
