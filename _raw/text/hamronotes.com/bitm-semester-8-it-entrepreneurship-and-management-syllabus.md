Pass Marks: 30

Credit Hours: 3

## Course Description

This course is structured for a BIM program and focuses on the fundamentals of entrepreneurship, including idea generation, market research, business model development, and financial management. The course will also cover legal and regulatory considerations, marketing strategies, leadership, and corporate social responsibility.

## Course Objective

The main objective of this course is to equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary to launch and manage successful IT-based startups. Through real-world case studies and experiential learning projects, students will gain practical insights into the challenges and opportunities in the ICT sector.
