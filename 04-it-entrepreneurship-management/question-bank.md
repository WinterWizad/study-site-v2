**BITM · IT Entrepreneurship and Management · IT248**  
**Full Marks:** 100 · **Pass Marks:** 50

### Group A — Brief answer questions [10 × 2 = 20]

1. Define any two types of entrepreneurships.
2. What is the concept of creative thinking?
3. Define business model.
4. What is intellectual property right?
5. Define business plan.
6. What is business eco system?
7. What is Risk Management?
8. Define Labor Law.
9. What is brand building?
10. Mention any two importance of CRM.

### Group B — Short answer questions (attempt any SIX) [6 × 5 = 30]

1. Discuss the role of ICT for effective entrepreneurship.
2. Explain the steps of prototyping development.
3. Explain any five building blocks of business model canvas framework.
4. Describe basic legal requirements for startups in Nepal.
5. Discuss the importance of ICT in developing a marketing strategy.
6. How ICT can be aligned with leadership characteristics? Explain with example.
7. “An implementation of an efficient CRM system leads to proper. customer retention”. Justify the given statement.

### Group C — Long answer questions (attempt any THREE) [3 × 10 = 30]

1. How different ICT Tools can be used for effective consumer insight? Explain with example.
2. What are the key components of cash flow management? How IT tools or software can be used for efficient cash flow management?
3. Describe the various types of revenue streams available to a business and explain the tools and importance of conducting a Cost Structure Analysis.
4. What are the legal challenges faced by Nepali startups in IT sector? As an IT manager propose a comprehensive strategy to address the legal challenges in IT sector.

### Group D — Comprehensive answer / case / situation analysis [20]

1. Analyze the following case carefully and answer the questions that follow:

> “You are appointed as the Lead Founder of a new technology-driven startup in Nepal. Drawing from the principles of entrepreneurship, provide a comprehensive roadmap for the venture's journey from conception to sustainable operation. Your answer must address the following four dimensions:
>
> a. Describe the Idea Development Process you would follow and how you would define your Target Market?
>
> b. How you would apply Digital Marketing techniques to acquire your customers?
>
> c. Identify three sources of funding relevant to the Nepalese context and explain the budgeting techniques you would use to ensure liquidity.
>
> d. Discuss which leadership style would be most effective for a startup environment and how you would manage conflicts within your team.”

## Part 5 — Exposure map: maximum, not guaranteed

The model question has optional questions. A unit's mapped exposure is the sum of every question that touches it. **It is not the number of marks the student will receive.** Because the candidate chooses six Group B and three Group C questions, the unit totals sum to more than 100.

| Unit | Topic | Hours | Mapped questions | Maximum mapped exposure |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Foundations of Entrepreneurship | 6 | A1, A2, B11, plus board A1/A6/B1 | 9 in model paper |
| 2 | Idea Generation and Market Research | 10 | A3, A4, B12, B13, B14, D22a-b | **29** in model paper |
| 3 | Business Model Development / Plan | 10 | A5, C21b, D22c | 12 in model paper |
| 4 | Legal and Regulatory Framework | 6 | A6, B15, D22d | 12 in model paper |
| 5 | Financial Management and Marketing | 10 | A7, A8, B16, C18, C20b | 24 in model paper |
| 6 | Leadership and CSR | 6 | A9, A10, B17, C19, C20a, C21a | **29** in model paper |

**Correct reading:** Units 2 and 6 each have a 29-mark ceiling in the model paper. Together they provide 58 marks of mapped exposure from 16 of 48 lecture hours. That makes them high priority, but it does **not** guarantee 58 marks.
