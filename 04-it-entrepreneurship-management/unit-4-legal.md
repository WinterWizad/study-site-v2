## 4.2 Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) (A6)

> **IPR are legal rights that protect creations of the mind — giving creators exclusive control over the use of their inventions, brands, code, and designs for a defined period.**

### The 4 main types (memorise for A6)

| Type | What it protects | Duration (Nepal) | IT startup example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patent** | inventions (new, inventive, industrially applicable) | 20 years from filing | novel algorithm, encryption method, hardware device |
| **Trademark** | brand identifiers (name, logo, slogan, sound) | 10 years, renewable indefinitely | app name, logo, tagline "Ride Safe" |
| **Copyright** | original literary, artistic, musical works (including code) | life of author + 50 years | source code, UI design, database, marketing content |
| **Industrial Design** | aesthetic appearance of a product | 10 years, renewable 5+5 | unique shape of a hardware IoT device |

### A6 · "What is intellectual property right (IPR)?" [2]

> IPR are legal rights protecting creations of the mind, giving creators exclusive control over their inventions, brands, code, and designs. Four main types: (1) **Patent** — novel inventions (algorithm, hardware), 20 years. (2) **Trademark** — brand identifiers (name, logo, slogan), 10 years renewable. (3) **Copyright** — original works including source code, UI, content, life + 50 years. (4) **Industrial Design** — aesthetic appearance of a product, 10 years renewable.

*(54 words. Definition + four named types with what they protect + duration.)*

## 4.4 Taxation basics for IT startups in Nepal (B15)

> **Nepal's tax regime for IT startups has specific incentives and compliance requirements.**

### The 5 tax heads (memorise for B15)

| Tax | Rate / Rule | Key points for IT startups |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate Income Tax (CIT)** | 25% standard; **20% for IT companies** (special industry) | file annually (Ashad end); advance tax quarterly |
| **Value Added Tax (VAT)** | 13% | mandatory registration if turnover > 50L; monthly returns; input credit on business expenses |
| **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)** | varies (1.5%–15%) | on payments: rent (10%), professional fees (15%), contractor (1.5%), salary (progressive) |
| **Advance Tax** | 25% of estimated annual tax | pay in 3 instalments (Poush, Chaitra, Ashad) |
| **Social Security Tax (SST)** | 20% (11% employer + 9% employee) | mandatory for all formal employees; goes to Social Security Fund (SSF) |

### IT-specific incentives (Income Tax Act, 2058)

| Incentive | Condition |
|---|---|
| **20% CIT** (vs 25% standard) | registered as IT industry, income from software/IT services |
| **100% tax holiday** (5 years) | for startups in Special Economic Zones (SEZ) or IT Parks |
| **Accelerated depreciation** | 50% first year on computers/software |
| **R&D deduction** | 150% deduction on qualifying R&D expenditure |

### B15 · "Explain taxation basics for IT startups in Nepal." [5]

> **Shape: five tax heads with rate/rule + two IT incentives. ~200 words.**

Nepal's taxation for IT startups covers five heads:

1. **Corporate Income Tax (CIT)** — 25% standard, but **20% for IT companies** (special industry). File annually (Ashad end); advance tax quarterly.
2. **Value Added Tax (VAT)** — 13%. Mandatory registration if turnover > 50 lakh. Monthly returns; input credit on business expenses.
3. **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)** — varies: rent 10%, professional fees 15%, contractor 1.5%, salary (progressive). Deduct at payment, deposit by 25th of next month.
4. **Advance Tax** — 25% of estimated annual tax, paid in three instalments (Poush, Chaitra, Ashad).
5. **Social Security Tax (SST)** — 20% total (11% employer + 9% employee). Mandatory for all formal employees; deposited to Social Security Fund.

**IT-specific incentives:** (1) **20% CIT rate** (vs 25%) for income from software/IT services. (2) **100% tax holiday for 5 years** for startups in SEZ/IT Parks. Also: accelerated depreciation (50% first year on computers/software) and 150% R&D deduction.

*(208 words)*

## 4.5 Compliance with Labour Laws

| Requirement | Labour Act, 2074 provision | Startup application |
|---|---|---|
| **Employment contract** | written, in Nepali/English, before joining | for all drivers (partners/employees) and staff |
| **Minimum wage** | notified annually | drivers: piece-rate + guaranteed minimum |
| **Working hours** | 8 hrs/day, 48 hrs/week; OT 1.5× | driver shifts; office staff compliance |
| **Leave** | 18 days annual, 12 days sick, 98 days maternity | accrual-based for employees |
| **Gratuity** | 8.33% of basic salary per year | accrual for employees; not for gig partners |
| **Provident Fund (PF)** | 10% employer + 10% employee | mandatory for employees; deposited monthly |
| **Health insurance** | mandatory (group policy) | for employees |
| **Termination** | notice (30 days) or compensation; valid grounds | performance, redundancy, misconduct |
| **Social Security Fund (SSF)** | 20% (11%+9%) replaces PF/gratuity/insurance | mandatory enrollment for all formal employees |

**Gig workers (drivers) vs employees:** the Act applies to "workers" — if driver is a partner (not employee), Labour Act may not fully apply, but **Social Security Tax (20%) still applies** if classified as worker. Current debate in Nepal courts.

## 4.6 Case sub-part (D22d) — "Complexities due to policy and law" [5]

> **Shape: 5 categories of legal/policy complexity for a ride-sharing app. ~150 words.**

Policy and law complexities for a Pathao-like ride-sharing app in Nepal:

1. **Company & transport regulation** — Pvt Ltd registration (OCR), transport permit (DoTM) for ride-sharing as a "contracted carriage" service; no clear "platform" category in Motor Vehicles Act.
2. **Labour classification** — drivers as "partners" vs "employees" — Labour Act 2074 applies to workers; SSF (20%) mandatory if employees; gig worker status legally untested in Nepal courts.
3. **Data privacy** — Individual Privacy Act 2075: consent for rider/driver data, purpose limitation, data minimisation, breach notification (72 hrs), DPO appointment, cross-border transfer restrictions.
4. **Tax compliance** — 20% CIT (IT rate), 13% VAT (if turnover > 50L), TDS on driver payouts (1.5% contractor), advance tax quarterly, SST 20% on driver earnings if classified as workers.
5. **Consumer protection & safety** — no dedicated ride-sharing regulation; liability for accidents unclear; insurance mandate (third-party + passenger); surge pricing transparency; dispute resolution mechanism.

*(168 words)*
