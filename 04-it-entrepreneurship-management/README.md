## The three things that decide this paper

1. **Units 2 and 6 have the largest mapped ceilings** — 29 marks each. These are maximum exposures, not guaranteed marks. Structure answers as definition → numbered points → Nepal example.
2. **Units 3 and 4 are recall-heavy but not automatic** — memorise the BMC, business-plan elements, IPR types, and legal frameworks. Tax rates and legal duties still need current verification.
3. **The case is 20 marks** — idea generation → market analysis → rider trust → policy and law. Learn the framework. The 2026 board paper used a generic startup case, so do not memorise only Pathao facts.
