**TRIBHUVAN UNIVERSITY — FACULTY OF MANAGEMENT — Office of the Dean**
**Model Question 2026**
**BIM / Eighth Semester / IT 248: Entrepreneurship and Management**
Full Marks: 100 · Pass Marks: 50 · Time: 3 Hours

Candidates are required to give their answers in their own words as far as practicable.

1. Define entrepreneurship.
2. What is an entrepreneurship ecosystem?
3. List any two techniques for creative thinking.
4. What do you mean by target market?
5. What is Business Model Canvas?

7. List any two funding sources for startups.
8. What is digital marketing?
9. Define leadership style.
10. What do you mean by corporate social responsibility (CSR)?

### Group "B" — Short answer questions (attempt any SIX) [6 × 5 = 30]

11. Explain any three key characteristics of successful entrepreneurs.
12. How do you identify market needs for a new IT idea?
13. Write a short note on competitor analysis.
14. Discuss the roles of trend analysis in idea generation.
15. Explain taxation basics for IT startups in Nepal.
16. What is the role of CRM in startups?
17. Explain the conflict resolution techniques within the team.

### Group "C" — Long answer questions (attempt any THREE) [3 × 10 = 30]

18. What are the different sources of funding for startups? Explain any three.
19. Explain different leadership styles used in startups.
20. How do you motivate employees in startups? Explain the digital marketing and sales techniques.
21. Discuss about ethical marketing strategies. What are the essential elements of a business plan?

### Group "D" — Comprehensive answer / case / situation analysis [20]

22. Analyze the following case carefully and answer the questions that follow:

> Suppose you are appointed as a promoting manager of a new ride-sharing app idea like Pathao in Nepal. Now express your idea to answer the following questions.
>
> a. Explain the steps you will follow for idea generation.
> b. How do you analyze the market?
> c. How do you ensure the benefit and trust to rider?
> d. What might be the complexities due to policy and law of our country?

> ⚠ **Two printing oddities, reproduced rather than corrected:**
> - **Q20** is two questions welded into one sentence ("motivate employees" is Unit 6; "digital marketing and sales techniques" is Unit 5). Answer it as two headed halves — that is almost certainly the intent, and it protects you either way.
> - **Q21** is likewise two: ethical marketing (Unit 6) and business plan elements (Unit 3). Same tactic.
> - The case calls the customer a **"rider"**; the syllabus case is Pathao. Use "rider" in your answer, not "customer", and the examiner sees you read it.

**Read the Unit 2 row.** Idea generation and market research carries the case plus four other questions — it is the single most examined unit in the paper, and it is also the unit students treat as "common sense". The case (D22) is answered almost entirely from Units 2 and 4: idea → market → trust → law.

**Unit 3 and Unit 4 are the cheap marks.** 12 marks each, 10 and 6 lecture hours, and every question in them is a list or a definition (BMC, business plan elements, IPR, taxation). They are also the units where a Nepali student has an unfair advantage, because the answers are about Nepal's actual law and tax regime.

## Group D shape

One 20-mark case with four sub-questions (a-d), 5 marks each. This is the **same shape as the BI board paper** and the opposite of the BI model paper — so a single case-method skeleton covers both IT 248 and IT 249.

The skeleton for this case:

1. **Restate the venture in one line** — "a ride-sharing platform in Kathmandu, competing with Pathao and inDrive".
2. **Answer a-d under their own headings**, in the paper's order.
3. **Name course concepts explicitly** — idea generation technique (brainstorming, SCAMPER, trend analysis), market analysis (TAM/SAM/SOM, competitor analysis, consumer behaviour), trust (escrow, verified drivers, insurance, rating, fare transparency), policy (company registration, labour law, transport regulation, data privacy, taxation).
4. **Close with a judgement**, not a summary: what would make this venture fail in Nepal, and what the promoting manager does about it first.

## How to use this file — the acceptance test

1. **Per unit, before calling it finished:** close the notes and answer that unit's rows out loud. A mapped question that cannot be answered from that unit's notes alone means the unit is **not** done.
2. **2-markers are not 1-markers.** Group A here carries double BI's weight per question and wants ~50-60 words: a definition plus one sentence of substance, or two named items with a half-line each. Writing a single sentence leaves marks on the table.
3. **5-mark Group B and 10-mark Group C are longer than the other subjects' equivalents.** Practise writing 5-markers at ~200 words and 10-markers at ~400 — not the 3/5-mark lengths that work for BI.
4. **Answer the doubled questions in halves.** Q20 and Q21 each contain two questions; treat the split as the marking scheme.
5. **Re-run the whole table as a mock** once all six units exist: 3 hours, no notes, all of Group A, one full Group B/C selection, and the case.

> ⚠ A model question is a **shape**, not a leak. The Pathao case is the syllabus's own named case study, which makes it likely but not certain — the 2026 board paper used a different venture (a technology startup founded by the candidate). Learn the four-part skeleton, not the Pathao facts.
