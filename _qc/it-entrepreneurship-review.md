## Standing notes for this subject

- Every 2-mark answer must be **counted** to 50–65 words, not eyeballed.
- Every 5-mark answer must be **counted** to 180–250 words.
- Every 10-mark answer must be **counted** to 350–450 words.
- The case (20 marks) must be **counted** to 700–900 words total.
- Measure final word counts **from the saved file**, never from a retyped copy.
- The model question is the checker — a unit is not done until its mapped model questions are answerable from that unit's notes alone.
- **2-markers are not 1-markers.** Group A carries double the weight per question vs BI/Cloud — write ~55 words: definition + one sentence of substance.
- **5-mark Group B and 10-mark Group C are longer than other subjects' equivalents.** Practise 5-markers at ~200 words, 10-markers at ~400 words.
- **Answer the doubled questions in halves.** Q20 (motivation + digital marketing/sales) and Q21 (ethical marketing + business plan elements) — treat the split as the marking scheme.
